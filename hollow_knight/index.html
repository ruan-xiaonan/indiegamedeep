Essential Tips

Master the Dash

The Mothwing Cloak (Dash) is the single most important ability. Get it early from the Mantis Lords area or by defeating Hornet in Greenpath. It dramatically improves both combat and exploration.

Focus on Masks First

Collect Mask Shards whenever possible. Each 4 shards give +1 Mask of health. More health means more mistakes you can survive while learning boss patterns.

Buy Maps Immediately

Find Cornifer in each area and buy the map. Then buy the Quill from Iselda to mark benches and stations. Wandering blind wastes enormous time in Hallownest.

Spells Are Safe Damage

Vengeful Spirit and Descending Dark deal big damage with invincibility frames. Use them during boss openings. Descending Dark's i-frames make it one of the best defensive tools in the game.

Farm Geo Early

The best early Geo farm is the Husk Guards in Forgotten Crossroads. Equip Dreamcatcher and you can also farm Essence for rewards from Seer. Later, the Colosseum is excellent.

Don't Fear Death

When you die, you leave a Shade at the spot. Return and strike it to recover all Geo. If you die again before recovering, the Geo is lost. Death is a teacher — learn boss patterns through repetition.

Nail Pogo Everything

Striking downward with your nail while airborne bounces you up. This "pogo" mechanic is essential for platforming and combat. Practice on spikes and enemies — it opens up sequence breaks and secret areas.

Listen for Humming

Cornifer hums when you're near his location. If you hear faint humming, explore nearby — he's selling a map for that area. The louder it gets, the closer you are.

Dream Nail for Lore

The Dream Nail lets you read the minds of NPCs and the dead. It's also required to access Dream Bosses (more difficult versions of some bosses) and the White Palace. Get it from the Resting Grounds.

Recommended Progression Order

Hollow Knight is a Metroidvania — you can explore freely, but this order minimizes backtracking and ensures you get key abilities before difficult areas.
