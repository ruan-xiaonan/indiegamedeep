New Mechanics

🧵

Silk System

Hornet uses Silk as a resource for abilities and healing. Unlike the Knight's Soul, Silk is gathered through combat and environmental interaction. It powers her signature moves and tools.

🔧

Crafting

A brand-new crafting system lets Hornet create weapons, tools, and traps from materials found across Pharloom. This adds a strategic layer not present in the original Hollow Knight.

🗡️

Weapon Variety

Unlike the Knight's single Nail, Hornet can wield multiple weapon types including her signature needle and thread. Different weapons may offer different attack patterns and ranges.

🪤

Trap System

Hornet can set traps using Silk and crafted items. This defensive tool allows for strategic combat setups, letting you lure enemies into prepared positions.

🏃

Faster Movement

Hornet is significantly more agile than the Knight. She runs faster, can grapple to surfaces, and has more aerial options. Platforming sections will be more dynamic and demanding.

🏆

Quest System

Silksong features a more formal quest system compared to the original. Hornet can take on tasks from NPCs across Pharloom, adding structure to exploration while maintaining freedom.
